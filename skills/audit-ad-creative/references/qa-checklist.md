# Creative QA checklist

## Blockers

- wrong product, color, price, offer, claim, legal copy, logo, or destination
- unapproved generated product representation
- missing required asset or target
- incorrect canvas ratio or dimensions
- unreadable or clipped legal text
- text overflow, missing font, broken image, or hidden critical layer
- important subject outside the platform safe zone
- duplicate export filename

## Needs review

- inferred font or OCR below high confidence
- inpainted area behind detailed product, face, hand, or pattern
- aggressive crop or image expansion
- weak contrast, hierarchy, or CTA clarity
- generated anatomy or fabric details that may be inaccurate
- large visual difference from the approved master

## Suggestions

- improve negative space or alignment
- simplify secondary decoration
- strengthen product prominence
- add more genuinely different PMax assets

Verify previews at actual size and at thumbnail size. A visually attractive frame does not pass if the product or offer is wrong.
