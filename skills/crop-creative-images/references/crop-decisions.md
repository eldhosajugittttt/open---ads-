# Crop decisions

Use this order:

1. **Safe crop:** remove only expendable environment.
2. **Art-directed crop:** move the focal point while retaining all protected content.
3. **Recompose:** move editable layout elements and resize the image region.
4. **Fit:** show the whole image and design the remaining canvas around it.
5. **Expand:** generate only the missing environment when a wider canvas is essential.

Treat these as protected regions:

- the entire advertised product and its distinctive construction;
- face, head, hands, feet, and meaningful pose;
- official logo when it is part of the source image;
- product interaction and important shadows;
- negative space reserved for copy;
- architectural or decorative lines that establish the composition.

Keep a practical breathing margin around protected content. Start with 5% of the shorter target dimension and increase it for faces, premium layouts, or large-format output.

Reject crops that create accidental amputations, edge tangencies, isolated floating objects, unreadable product silhouettes, or a different product impression.
