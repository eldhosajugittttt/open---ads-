# Generate Product Imagery

Generate fewer, stronger master images and reuse them across compositions.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/product-truth.md`.
2. Identify the exact product-truth image and the hardest target ratio.
3. Decide between safe compositing and full reference-guided generation.
4. Use the product image only as product truth; use mood references only for pose, lighting, environment, and camera direction.
5. Generate image-only output with protected focal points and copy-safe negative space.
6. Review product color, silhouette, material, pattern, construction, anatomy, and scene artifacts. If a logo is physically printed, embroidered, or embossed on the product, compare its shape, spelling, orientation, scale, and placement against the approved product image.
7. Approve one master before creating expensive final variations.
8. Store provenance, prompt, source assets, and review state.

## Fast default

Generate low-cost previews in parallel, approve one, and create the final only for the winner. Reuse the approved master through intentional crops or image extension. Do not generate a new scene for every ad size.

Keep all typography, prices, badges, CTAs, legal content, and standalone official logos in Figma or the final layout system. Never ask image generation to recreate a standalone brand logo.
