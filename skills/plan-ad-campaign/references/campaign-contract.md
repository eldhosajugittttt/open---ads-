# Campaign contract

Required campaign information:

- `campaign_id`, client, locale, objective, audience, and deadline
- master concept name, headline, CTA, product asset, and destination URL
- optional subheadline, offer, price, legal copy, and official logo asset
- target placement IDs from the versioned placement registry
- approval state for concept, product truth, offer, legal, and publishing

Treat these as approval-sensitive: prices, discounts, availability dates, product properties, certifications, comparative claims, warranties, legal copy, and destination URLs.

For PMax, provide at minimum three headlines, one long headline, two descriptions, and a verified business name. At least one headline must fit the short-headline constraint in the current platform registry.

Prefer one coherent asset group per product category or campaign theme. Do not combine unrelated audiences or product families merely to create more variations.
